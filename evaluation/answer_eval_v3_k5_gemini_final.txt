Q8 [direct] How do database automations work?

  A: Database automations function as sequences of actions that automatically occur whenever a specific change takes place within a database. Q9 [direct] How can I set a reminder in Notion?

You can also create reminders in a database as long as the database has a Date property.

Q70 [direct] What is a restricted member, and how are they different from a regular member?
  expected: whos-who-in-a-workspace.md | status: answered | score: 0.90 | corrected: None
  A: Based on the provided context, restricted members share many capabilities with regular workspace members—such as creating private pages, using Notion AI, logging in with SSO, and being added to permission groups—and they affect billing in the same way. 

However, they differ from regular members in the following ways:
* They need to be added to specific teamspaces and pages (including default teamspaces) to access them.
* They cannot create teamspaces.
* They can only share pages with members who have been added to the same teamspaces and pages as them.
* They can only @-mention members with whom they share access to a page or teamspace.
* They won't see Connect in Settings and cannot use Connect to link other apps to Notion.
* Additionally, controls related to restricted members can be found in Settings, and teamspace member roles encompass both regular and restricted members so that a restricted member cannot automatically have a lower level of access than a regular member unless permission groups or individual permissions are used.
